Where it started

Since 2001, the taste of Ecuador, wherever we go.

Our encebollado has been celebrated as the best in Europe. Now, we bring that authentic passion to 339 Miracle Mile in Coral Gables.

In Coral Gables, our team makes every dish from scratch, honoring the original ingredients: fresh green plantain, roasted peanuts, cilantro, yuca and red onion.

  • MiamiNow Open on Miracle Mile
  • BarcelonaOriginal House
  • ZaragozaHistoric Quarter
  • MadridTemporarily Closed

Seven houses across four international cities.

Signature Dish 01

Encebollado

A fragrant albacore tuna and yuca broth finished with pickled red onions and fresh cilantro. The iconic bowl that built our international reputation.

Fritada
